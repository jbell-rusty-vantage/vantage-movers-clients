## Two journeys followed beyond the directory

From Florida, the Boca Raton page narrows the planning questions to housing, access, climate and paperwork. For Madison, commission one local page a resident could use before delivery. Include a campus-area example with its geographic limits, an official city link and a question to ask the building. Keep the Wisconsin page useful to people moving elsewhere in the state. [Boca Raton city page](https://safeshipmoving.com/locations/florida-movers/boca-raton-movers/)

From the articles footer, the packing article moves from a short summary through categories of belongings to a practical set-aside area and a carrier-specific check. Adapt that editorial sequence for the inventory checklist: room list, unusual items, access notes, then questions for the coordinator. Have customers consult their assigned carrier's written restrictions. [Packing article](https://safeshipmoving.com/articles/what-not-to-pack-when-moving/)

## Initial authoritative sources for writers

| Decision or claim | Authority and scope | Editorial instruction |
|---|---|---|
| Who arranges and who transports an interstate household shipment? | [FMCSA on movers and brokers](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/movers-vs-brokers) | Explain the two roles. Confirm Vantage’s exact name, current identifiers, disclosure and contact details against its approved business records and registration before publishing. |
| How can the customer compare written estimates? | [FMCSA moving checklist](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/moving-checklist) · [Consumer handbook](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/consumer-protection/your-rights-and-responsibilities-when-you-move) | Base worksheet fields on comparable inventory, included services, charges and terms. Refer readers to the written estimate for their own shipment. |
| How do I check a mover or reach my carrier? | [Federal mover lookup](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/search-mover) · [Vantage consumer information](https://www.vantagehomemovers.com/consumer-information) | Keep registration lookup distinct from finding the carrier assigned to an individual move. Check the company against the shipment paperwork. |
| What changes after arrival in Wisconsin? | [Wisconsin DOT new residents](https://wisconsindot.gov/Pages/dmv/vehicles/title-plates/new-res-default.aspx) | Link to the state’s license and vehicle instructions. Recheck deadlines and eligibility before quoting specific requirements. |
| What is distinctive about moving near campus in Madison? | [City of Madison August student move-out](https://www.cityofmadison.com/streets/trash-recycling/move-out-clean-outs/august-student-move-out) | The published schedule is seasonal and area-specific. Use the city’s current schedule for the relevant year. Ask the building directly about elevators and loading access. |
| What can a newcomer explore? | [City of Madison Lake Monona waterfront project](https://www.cityofmadison.com/parks/projects/madison-lakeway-lake-monona-waterfront) | Distinguish planned improvements from places currently open. Check current access before suggesting an outing. |

Maintain one ledger entry per material claim: page and locale, proposed wording, source and section, geographic scope, date checked, reviewer, and recheck trigger. For commercial claims also record the Vantage document or operating owner who confirmed them. Link readers directly to official resources. Remove unsupported claims or turn them into questions a customer can ask. Never infer Vantage equipment ownership, guaranteed dates, intrastate availability, insurance terms, savings or booking volume from a competitor page.
